Most people never finish. Writing a full thought in Devanagari or Tamil on a phone is slow enough that the thought gets shortened, or it simply never gets posted — and the person quietly decides they are someone who reads rather than someone who writes.

The apps that do translate usually translate the content and leave an English interface around it. Navigation, prompts and layout stay foreign. It never feels like a product made for you. It feels like a product made for someone else, that you are allowed to use.

It doesn’t just write it down. It does it.

Dictation drops your words in a text box and leaves you there. Say post this, find that thread from last week, remind me tomorrow morning — and the thing you asked for is already done when you look up.

You choose how much rope it gets. Ask and Echo only talks back. Do and it can act on your behalf — and anything that writes, posts or spends stops and shows you a confirmation card first. It never takes an action you did not watch it take.
