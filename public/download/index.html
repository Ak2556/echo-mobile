Your phone will try to stop you. It will say something like “Chrome is not allowed to install unknown apps”. It says that about every app that did not come from the Play Store, including the ones you already trust. Tap Settings, allow it once, tap Install.

It takes about a minute on a normal connection and asks for nothing while it installs. Echo asks for the microphone the first time you hold the button, and for nothing else until you use the part that needs it.

It uninstalls like anything else. Hold the icon, remove, gone — and if you delete your account first, what you wrote goes with it.
